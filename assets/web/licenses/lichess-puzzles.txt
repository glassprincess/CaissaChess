puzzles.json contains 600 original puzzle records from bounded pages of the
official mirror, retrieved on 2026-11-25, with rating 600–2400. The initial
60 records use popularity >=70; the added 540 use popularity >=75, at least
100 plays, and rating deviation <=110. Every complete line is legal.
Each record links to its Lichess puzzle and original game. The FEN is before
the opponent's setup move. The solution begins after that setup move.
The application validates the legality of every bundled line at load time.
No full database download or external connection is required during play.
When online, the app requests https://lichess.org/api/puzzle/next anonymously.
Validated online records are cached locally, with bundled fallback on failure.
